About

A broke teenager who's passionate about a lot of things but is too broke to try them all

I'm a high school student from Casablanca building at the crossroads of robotics, software, and community organizing. I compete with FTC team NOVATARD #24950 — two-time Inspire Award winners — and organize hackathons that have become the largest teen-led events of their kind in Morocco. The four events I've co-organized — Counterspell, Scrapyard, Daydream, and Campfire — collectively brought together over 380 participants and hold four of the top five spots in Morocco's national teen-led hackathon ranking. They all started with a simple idea: run the kind of event we wished existed.
